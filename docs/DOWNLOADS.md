# 作品与下载

**用自己的小说开始：**[下载 AI 创作入门包（Prompt Starter）](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/download/v0.9.5/taleprism-agent-starter.zip)。解压后把一章原文和模板交给自己的 Agent，[从第一条指令开始](../prompts/START-HERE.md)。不需要先装制作台。

**要装本地制作台：**下载[制作台源码包](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/download/v0.9.5/taleprism-workbench-source.zip)，按[安装说明](快速上手.md)操作。校验清单只是可选的验证文件。

**想看成品：**[在线读《赤壁》](https://ethereal-space-one.github.io/taleprism/)，无需下载。 两个作品包沿用既有读本；手动声景修复适用于 v0.9.5 制作台新导出的版本。

**想拆解、修改或导出：**从 [v0.9.5 发布页](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/tag/v0.9.5) 按作品选包。新版作品包解压一次即可阅读：打开 `Reader/index.html`；要接着做，在制作台导入同包的 `Project.zip`，不是外层 ZIP。读本与工程对应同一作品版本。如需历史版本，请使用各作品注明的历史链接。

## 赤壁 / Chibi

**[下载《赤壁》作品包](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/download/v0.9.5/taleprism-chibi-work.zip)**：读本和可编辑工程放在同一个 ZIP。群像、图解和原文段落对齐的示例。可先[在线阅读](https://ethereal-space-one.github.io/taleprism/)，也可从[历史 v0.9.2 发布页](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/tag/v0.9.2)取得旧的《赤壁》离线读本或可编辑工程。

## 末班灯 / Last Light

**[下载《末班灯》作品包](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/download/v0.9.5/taleprism-last-light-work.zip)**：读本和可编辑工程放在同一个 ZIP。三镜头入门，适合练习场景和道具连续性。看[制作教程](../examples/last-light/README.md)，或从[历史 v0.9.2 发布页](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/tag/v0.9.2)取得旧的读本或工程。

## 仅试读 / Reader-only

[《留门》](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/download/v0.9.2/taleprism-v0.9.2-the-door-left-open-reader.zip)与[《留白处》](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/download/v0.9.2/taleprism-v0.9.2-the-margin-reader.zip)只有离线读本，没有公开可编辑工程。下载并完整解压，打开 `index.html`。

## Works and downloads

**Use your own novel:** Get the [AI Author Starter](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/download/v0.9.5/taleprism-agent-starter.zip) and bring a chapter to [your chosen agent](../prompts/START-HERE.md#english-walkthrough). No workbench required for your first pass.

**Read now:** [Open Chibi online](https://ethereal-space-one.github.io/taleprism/). The two work bundles keep their existing readers; the manual-audio fix applies to new v0.9.5 workbench exports. The two [reader-only examples](#仅试读--reader-only) remain offline downloads.

**Read and edit an example:** Download [Chibi](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/download/v0.9.5/taleprism-chibi-work.zip) or [Last Light](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/download/v0.9.5/taleprism-last-light-work.zip) as one bundle per work. Unzip once, open `Reader/index.html`, or import the included `Project.zip` in the workbench. The original downloads remain available in [v0.9.2](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/tag/v0.9.2).

**Install the local workbench:** Download the [workbench source](https://github.com/Ethereal-Space-one/taleprism/releases/download/v0.9.5/taleprism-workbench-source.zip) and follow [workbench setup](快速上手.md). GitHub's automatic source snapshots are not works or starter kits. Checksums and manifest are optional verification files.
